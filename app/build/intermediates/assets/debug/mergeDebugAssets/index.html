Cutting parameters

Spindle speed, feed rate, material removal rate, machining time and cutting power.

Power = Fc · Vc / 60. Assumes a sharp tool and full depth of cut.

Bearing life (ISO 281 basic rating life)

L10 is the life reached by 90% of an identical bearing group.

Lnm = a1 · L10, a1 from ISO 281. Life assumes clean lubrication and correct mounting.
